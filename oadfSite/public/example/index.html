Example

One club evening at Trilofos Archery. Maria shoots a WA 18 at 18 metres. The files below are the oracle for version 1.

Start at oadf.yaml. Paths inside documents are relative to that directory. Elena coaches, Nikos coaches and shoots, and the session records a scored card, a bulk count, equipment revisions, sensor series, and photos.

The story’s local clock is UTC+2. Every timestamp in the files is UTC with a Z suffix. The photographs are 1×1 placeholders so the paths resolve.
